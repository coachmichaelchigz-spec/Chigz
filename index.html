Welcome to Monate

A calm, affordable retreat in Tzaneen

Monate Guest House is a peaceful and budget-friendly home away from home, tucked into the leafy heart of Medipark, Tzaneen. Whether you're travelling for work, passing through Limpopo, or visiting family, we offer the comfort, quiet and security you need to rest easy.

Every room comes with its own en-suite bathroom and TV, while our secure parking, cosy bar area and softly-lit garden make it easy to unwind after a long day. Friendly service, fair prices and a great location — that's the Monate promise.
