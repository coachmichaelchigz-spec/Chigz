Welcome to Monate

A Warm, Restful Home Away From Home

Tucked away on Hospital Street in the leafy Medipark suburb, Monate Guest House offers the kind of peaceful, unhurried stay that's hard to find. Whether you're in Tzaneen for business, family, or a getaway through Limpopo, you'll feel instantly at ease.

Our comfortable en-suite rooms, secure parking and tranquil garden make Monate the affordable choice that never compromises on comfort. Relax with refreshments at the bar, unwind under the soft glow of our garden lighting, and wake up ready to explore.
